Privacy Policy

This policy covers these Android apps by Snug Puzzle Studio:
• Nap Spots: Cat Logic Puzzle (package com.ravisanchala.napspots)
• Sudoku by Snug Puzzle Studio (package com.ravisanchala.sudoku)
In this policy, "the app" means any of these apps.
Developer: Snug Puzzle Studio (Ravikumar J Sanchala), Rajkot, Gujarat, India
Effective date: 17 November 2026 (updated 17 November 2026 to add Sudoku)

Summary

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your game progress (levels, stars, score, streak, unlocked cats, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect some device data to show and measure ads, as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress, statistics and settings locally on your phone so you can continue where you left off. This information is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show rewarded ads (for example "watch an ad to get an extra heart, hints or another chance") and occasional full-screen ads between levels or games. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Device advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)Showing ads, frequency capping, measurement and, where permitted, personalised ads
IP address (used to estimate general location, such as country)Ad delivery, fraud prevention, legal compliance
Device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, language)Ad delivery and diagnostics
Ad interactions (ads shown, taps, rewards earned)Measurement, reporting and fraud prevention
Crash and performance diagnostics of the ads SDKKeeping ads working reliably

This data is collected and processed by Google as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Your choices and consent

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: when you first open the app, a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) asks for your choices about personalised ads. You can change them at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy & ad choices.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • If you buy "Remove ads" (when available), full-screen ads between levels or games are turned off. Optional rewarded ads may still be offered.

4. In-app purchases

If optional in-app purchases are offered, they are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Google Play tells the app only whether a purchase was made, so it can unlock the item.

5. Children

Our apps are designed for a general audience of adults and teenagers and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data retention and deletion

We do not keep any personal data on our own servers. Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data. For data processed by Google for advertising, see Google's policies linked above, or reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

7. Security

We use Google's official SDKs and keep the app updated. Data sent by the ads SDK to Google is encrypted in transit.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
